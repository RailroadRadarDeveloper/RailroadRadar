Report an Issue

Tell us what happened. Reports are private and reviewed by authorized RailroadRadar administrators.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Only provide contact details if you want us to be able to respond.

For anonymous reports, RailroadRadar does not add your name, email, or account ID to the submission. Firebase and hosting providers may process technical connection data under their own policies. If you enter contact information above, it will be included so we can reply.

To report a train/map issue, account problem, or feature issue, please open the Live Map in another tab to sign in. Safety and privacy concerns can be submitted anonymously.
